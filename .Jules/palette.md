## 2025-02-18 - Disable primary action buttons based on prerequisite table presence
**Learning:** Clicking on action buttons (like running a cross-check) when the backend tables aren't present yet leads to an error or confusion. Explaining via tooltips why buttons are disabled helps users understand the required order of operations.
**Action:** Always dynamically calculate button `disabled` states for major actions depending on whether their prereq data exists, and provide clear `help` tooltips guiding users to upload the required data.
