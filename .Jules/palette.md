## 2025-05-05 - Streamlit Bulk Assign Tooltips & Placeholders
**Learning:** Adding descriptive `placeholder` attributes to text inputs and utilizing `help` parameters on both inputs and buttons significantly clarifies the intent of potentially destructive actions (like mass assignment where an empty field deletes data).
**Action:** Always include clear tooltips (`help`) and placeholders on Streamlit forms that perform destructive or bulk actions to explicitly inform the user of the consequences before they interact.
